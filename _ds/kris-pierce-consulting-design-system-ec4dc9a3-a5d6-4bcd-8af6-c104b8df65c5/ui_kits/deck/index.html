Kris Pierce Consulting

Consumer and community engagement strategy for organisations that need patients, carers, and communities at the centre of their work.

What we do

Consumer and community engagement across health, research, and rare disease.

Kris Pierce Consulting helps organisations build genuine, sustained relationships with the people their work is meant to serve. We design and deliver engagement that goes beyond consultation, embedding consumer and community voices into governance, research design, and programme delivery.

The practice works across sectors where meaningful participation changes outcomes: pharmaceuticals, research institutions, and community organisations.

Sectors we work in

Three focus areas

⚲

Pharmaceutical and biotech

Patient engagement strategy for clinical development, regulatory submissions, and market access. Helping sponsors bring the patient perspective into trial design and evidence generation.

⚙

Research and academia

Co-designed research frameworks, consumer advisory structures, and participatory methods. Supporting investigators to embed lived experience from protocol through to publication.

♡

Not-for-profit and advocacy

Governance design, community engagement strategy, and capacity building for patient organisations and advocacy groups navigating complex health and policy landscapes.

How we work

Flexible methodology, tailored to context

01

Workshops

Facilitated sessions that bring stakeholders together to align on priorities, surface insights, and build shared understanding across organisational silos.

02

Co-design

Participatory design processes where consumers and communities shape programmes, services, and research alongside the teams delivering them.

03

Focus groups

Structured qualitative engagement with patients, carers, and community members to gather perspectives that inform strategy and decision making.

04

Engagement frameworks

Repeatable structures for sustained consumer participation: advisory committees, feedback loops, and governance models that outlast any single project.
