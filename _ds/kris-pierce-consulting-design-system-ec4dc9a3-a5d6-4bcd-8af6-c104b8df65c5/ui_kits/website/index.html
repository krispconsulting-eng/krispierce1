About

Kris Pierce

I work with health organisations, government agencies, and community groups to design engagement that actually works. Not the glossy kind that sits in a report. The kind where consumers shape strategy, governance, and service design from the start.

With more than two decades across the Australian health system, I bring both the strategic lens and the operational detail. I have led consumer partnership programmes for peak bodies, built community governance structures for primary health networks, and facilitated co-design processes that changed how services are delivered.

Based in Australia, I work nationally and internationally with organisations ready to move beyond tick-box consultation.
