# Rare disease training

A working repository for designing and running rare disease capability courses.
One source menu, two built suites (one for families and advocates, one for
patient organisations), and a sample face-to-face agenda for each.

## Contents

- **`subject-repository.md`**: the source menu. A catalogue of the recurring
  subjects across the major rare disease patient-training programs worldwide
  (EURORDIS, EUPATI, EUCAPA, Global Genes RDDS, Genetic Alliance UK, Rare Voices
  Australia, and the Australian consumer-and-community-involvement
  infrastructure), grouped into ten domains with audience, format and source
  tags. Select and re-source from this; do not read it as a fixed curriculum.

- **`course-suite-families-and-advocates.md`**: the built suite for individuals.
  Six runnable courses across three tiers, scoped to families and advocates,
  each deliverable both online and face to face.

- **`course-suite-organisations.md`**: the built suite for groups. Seven runnable
  courses across four tiers, scoped to the people running or building a patient
  organisation, each deliverable both online and face to face.

- **`event-blueprint-families-and-advocates-two-day.md`**: a sample two-day
  face-to-face agenda assembling the families-and-advocates suite.

- **`event-blueprint-org-two-day.md`**: a sample two-day face-to-face agenda
  assembling the organisations suite.

## Two suites, one menu

The two suites split the audience the way the repository does. The
families-and-advocates suite builds individuals: understanding the system, then
partnering in research, regulation and HTA, then influencing it. The
organisations suite builds groups: governance, funding, community, data assets
and sector influence. A subject like systemic advocacy appears in both, pitched
at the individual in one and the organisation in the other. Keep a person in the
suite that matches their responsibility.

### Families and advocates

| Tier | Course | Learner |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation | Understanding the rare disease system | Families |
| Partnering | From lived experience to evidence | Advocates |
| Partnering | Inside research and clinical trials | Advocates |
| Partnering | Access, regulation and HTA in Australia | Advocates |
| Influence | Advocacy that moves the system | Advocates |
| Woven strand | Sustaining yourself in this work | Both |

### Organisations

| Tier | Course | Best entry point |
|---|---|---|
| Foundations | Governance, compliance and running an organisation | New or forming groups |
| Sustainability | Funding and sustainability | Groups needing to fund the work |
| Sustainability | Building and sustaining community | Any group serving a community |
| Research readiness | Data assets and research readiness | Groups near research or industry |
| Research readiness | Making the case to partners and investors | Groups seeking partnership or investment |
| Influence | Systemic advocacy and ecosystem building | Groups ready to shape the system |
| Woven strand | Leading without burning out | Every group, always |

## Delivery in one line

Every course in both suites runs blended online (self-paced modules plus live
application sessions, at three depths) or as a stream inside a face-to-face
event. A community of practice, alumni network and mentoring turn a finished
course into a capability that keeps growing across the sector.

## Jurisdiction

Australian throughout: ACNC for charity governance, TGA for regulation, PBAC and
MSAC for reimbursement and HTA, the National Strategic Action Plan for Rare
Diseases for policy, the NHMRC Statement on Consumer and Community Involvement for
research involvement, and the Medicines Australia Code of Conduct for any
industry-facing delivery.
