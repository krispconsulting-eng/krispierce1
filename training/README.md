# Rare disease training

A working repository for designing and running rare disease capability courses.
Two files, one input and one output, plus a sample face-to-face agenda.

## Contents

- **`subject-repository.md`**: the source menu. A catalogue of the recurring
  subjects across the major rare disease patient-training programmes worldwide
  (EURORDIS, EUPATI, EUCAPA, Global Genes RDDS, Genetic Alliance UK, Rare Voices
  Australia, and the Australian consumer-and-community-involvement
  infrastructure), grouped into ten domains with audience, format and source
  tags. Select and re-source from this; do not read it as a fixed curriculum.

- **`course-suite-families-and-advocates.md`**: the built suite. A slice of the
  menu turned into six runnable courses across three tiers, scoped to families
  and advocates, each deliverable both online and face to face.

- **`event-blueprint-two-day.md`**: a sample two-day face-to-face agenda that
  assembles the suite into a live programme.

## The suite at a glance

| Tier | Course | Learner |
|---|---|---|
| Orientation | Understanding the rare disease system | Families |
| Partnering | From lived experience to evidence | Advocates |
| Partnering | Inside research and clinical trials | Advocates |
| Partnering | Access, regulation and HTA in Australia | Advocates |
| Influence | Advocacy that moves the system | Advocates |
| Woven strand | Sustaining yourself in this work | Both |

## Delivery in one line

Each course runs blended online (self-paced modules plus live application
sessions, at three depths) or as a stream inside a face-to-face event. A
community of practice, alumni network and mentoring turn a finished course into a
capability that keeps growing.

## Jurisdiction

Australian throughout: TGA for regulation, PBAC and MSAC for reimbursement and
HTA, the National Strategic Action Plan for Rare Diseases for policy, the NHMRC
Statement on Consumer and Community Involvement for research involvement, and the
Medicines Australia Code of Conduct for any industry-facing delivery.
