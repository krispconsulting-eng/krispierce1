Why it is credible

Taught from both sides of the table

Rare Intelligence is a program of Kris Pierce Consulting. Kris has been a carer in rare disease for two decades, and has held senior advisory roles in health technology assessment, genomics and consumer engagement. The courses carry both: the lived experience, and the machinery of how decisions actually get made.

The content is not a generic overview. It is distilled from the major international patient-training programs, EURORDIS, EUPATI, EUCAPA, Global Genes, Genetic Alliance UK and Rare Voices Australia, then anchored to the Australian system throughout: the TGA, the PBAC and MSAC, the National Strategic Action Plan for Rare Diseases, and the NHMRC involvement standards.

There is no launch-week urgency here and no manufactured scarcity. The courses are worth the price because of what you can do at the end of them.

Content that stays current

Current as at July 2026

Most online courses are right on the day they publish and slowly go out of date. These do not.

  • A regulatory watch on the TGA, PBAC, MSAC, ACNC and the National Action Plan.
  • Every lesson linked to the rules it depends on, so a change is found and fixed fast.
  • A visible date on every course and on your certificate for when the content was last checked.
