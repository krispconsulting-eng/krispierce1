# Kris Pierce Consulting: Brand Guidelines v3.0

## 1. Brand Identity

**Practice name:** Kris Pierce Consulting
**Website:** krispierce.com.au
**Contact:** info@krispierce.com.au

This is a consulting practice focused on consumer and community engagement across health, research, and rare disease. It is not a charity, not a coaching brand, and not a motivational platform. The tone is peer to peer: one specialist talking to another.


## 2. Voice and Copy Rules

- **Australian English** throughout (organisation, recognise, programme as program)
- **No em dashes, anywhere.** Use commas, colons, semicolons, brackets, or full stops instead
- Peer to peer, not coach. No motivational or salesy tone
- One idea per piece
- Outcome-first: lead with what changes, not with methodology
- The live site (krispierce.com.au) is the source of truth for all copy

## 8. Motion and Easing

### Easing Curves

| Name | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Ease Out** | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | General transitions, panel entrances |
| **Ease In-Out** | `cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)` | Symmetrical animations |
| **Premium** | `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` | Button transforms, nav transitions |
| **Text Reveal** | `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` | Hero text stagger, content reveals |

### Durations

| Context | Duration |
|---------|----------|
| Micro (hover, active) | 200ms to 300ms |
| Transitions (border, background) | 250ms to 400ms |
| Panel/content reveals | 700ms to 900ms |
| Animated blob float | 12s to 18s (infinite, ease-in-out) |

### Hero Text Reveal (stagger)

Overline, title, lead, and CTA animate in sequence with increasing delay:
- Child 1: 0.2s delay
- Child 2: 0.35s delay
- Child 3: 0.5s delay
- Child 4: 0.65s delay

Animation: `translateY(24px) + opacity 0` to `translateY(0) + opacity 1`, 0.9s, text-reveal easing.

### Button Interactions

- **Hover:** `translateY(-2px)` + shadow bloom
- **Active:** `translateY(0) scale(0.985)` (tactile press)
- All skipped under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`

## 10. Component Patterns

### Buttons

**Primary (Blue):**
Background: Blue 600 (`#3E6F8E`). Text: Cream 50. Pill radius (999px). Padding: 14px 28px. Font: Archivo, 15px, weight 600. Hover: translateY(-2px) + blue shadow. Active: scale(0.985).

**Outline Light (on dark):**
Transparent background. Cream 50 text. Border: `rgba(251,250,247, 0.4)`. Hover: border becomes Blue 400, text becomes Blue 200.

### Overline / Eyebrow Tag

Archivo, 12px, weight 600, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.2em. Colour: Teal 600 on light backgrounds, Blue 200 on dark.

### Cards

White (`#FFFFFF`) on cream. Border: `border-subtle`. Radius: `lg` (24px) or `xl` (32px). Shadow: `card`. Hover: translateY(-2px) + shadow-md.

### Nav

Fixed position, transparent on page load. Scrolled state: Forest background at 92% opacity, backdrop-blur 14px. Light variant (interior pages): dark text and dark logo before scroll, transitions to cream on forest.

### Decorative Elements (home hero only)

**Animated blobs:** 3 radial gradient circles, positioned absolute, floating on 12s/15s/18s infinite cycles.
- Blob 1: Blue-tinted `rgba(127,168,194, 0.5)`, top-right
- Blob 2: Teal-tinted `rgba(207,224,218, 0.28)`, bottom-left
- Blob 3: Deep-blue `rgba(63,111,142, 0.18)`, centre

**SVG arc circles:** 3 concentric circles centred at (1180, 250) with radii 120/200/290, stroked in blue/teal at descending opacity (0.55, 0.32, 0.22). Stroke-width: 1.5.


## 11. Page Structure

### Home Page

Full-height forest gradient hero (min-height 88vh) with animated blobs, SVG arcs, Outfit weight-200 headline, lead text, and two CTA buttons.

### Interior Pages

Compact cream-background header (120px top padding, 40px bottom padding) with overline, title, and optional lead text. Subtle bottom border. No gradient, no blobs, no arcs.
