**Subject:** Patient voice in [project/programme name]

Dear [name],

[Organisation]'s work on [specific project, programme, or priority] caught my attention, and it prompted me to reach out.

My work helps health organisations generate insights and reach patient-centred outcomes. The methodology depends on the challenge: participatory research, co-design, structured discovery, whatever surfaces what you actually need to know.

One thing that comes up often: engagement gets commissioned to satisfy a funder or a governance requirement, then sits in a report without changing the decision it was meant to inform. The work that lands is the work designed from the outset to feed a real choice.

If patient and carer voice is something you're working through on [project], it may be worth a short conversation. No obligation, and I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right fit.

## Cold Outreach 2 — Industry Partner (Pharma / MedTech)

**Subject:** Patient perspective ahead of [launch / submission / programme]

Dear [name],

[Company] is [preparing for launch / working through an HTA submission / building a patient programme] in [therapeutic area], and patient and carer perspective is often the part that's hardest to get right. That's the reason for this note.

My work brings patient insight into evidence, strategy, and launch for industry partners, in a way that's credible and compliant. The Medicines Australia Code of Conduct is familiar ground, and the work stays inside it, not around it.

The risk that comes up most is authenticity. Engagement bolted on to satisfy a requirement reads as exactly that, to consumers and to the bodies assessing your evidence. Done properly, it strengthens the submission and the strategy, and meets the compliance bar as a byproduct.

If this is live for you, I'd welcome a conversation about what genuine, compliant engagement could look like for [company].
