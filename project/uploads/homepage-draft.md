## Who I work with

Three kinds of organisation, and the brief is rarely the same twice.

Health organisations and research teams come when patient voice needs to be more than a line in a grant application. Hospitals, research institutes, and NFPs that want engagement built into the work, not bolted on at the end.

Industry partners, pharmaceutical and MedTech, come when they're preparing for launch, navigating reimbursement, or working out how to bring patient experience into evidence and strategy without crossing a line. The Medicines Australia Code of Conduct shapes that work, and I work inside it, not around it.

Not-for-profits and advocacy groups come when they want their engagement to carry real weight: with members, with funders, with the bodies that make decisions about their community.

## How I work

No fixed method. The method follows the question.

**Participatory research and discovery.** When you need to understand what people actually experience, not what a survey assumes they experience. Focus groups, interviews, and structured discovery that generate insight you can act on.

**Co-design and solution development.** Building the answer with the people affected, not consulting them on one already drafted. The difference shows up later, in whether the solution survives contact with reality.

**Strategic insights.** What people say, turned into something an organisation can use: themes, priorities, and the outcomes that genuinely matter to the people at the centre.

**Industry support.** Patient voice brought into evidence, HTA submissions, and launch strategy in a way that's credible and compliant.

**Ongoing engagement.** Retained advisory work for organisations that want this thinking in the room continuously, not project by project.

Methodology flexible. Outcome-focused. The point is never the method. The point is what changes because of it.
