What I do

Four ways the practice supports decision-makers in complex health environments.

01

Strategic consulting

Embedding lived experience into policy, research, and system design so decisions are informed, feasible, and grounded in real-world conditions.

02

Consumer & patient engagement

Designing meaningful, evidence-based engagement approaches that move beyond tokenism and influence the decisions that matter.

03

Health systems & HTA

Supporting evidence readiness and consumer integration across regulatory, reimbursement, and clinical decision-making.

04

Speaking & facilitation

Translating complexity into clear, actionable insight for boards, committees, conferences, and cross-sector working groups.

Why it matters

Health systems are not designed for complexity.

Patients and families are often required to navigate what the system cannot see.

I work at the intersection of lived experience and system design, ensuring decisions are grounded in reality, not assumption, and that engagement is structured to change something, not simply to be recorded.
