# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Health, research, and rare-disease organisations (and the people who run them) who need consumer and community engagement expertise. The visitor is typically a peer decision-maker — a program lead, researcher, or funder — evaluating whether to bring Kris Pierce Consulting in, not a general public audience being "sold to."

## Product Purpose

The website for Kris Pierce Consulting (KrispConsulting), a consulting practice focused on consumer and community engagement across health, research, and rare disease. It exists to establish credibility and generate consulting inquiries. Success looks like a visitor understanding the specific outcomes Kris delivers and reaching out, without ever feeling "marketed to."

## Brand Personality

Peer, not coach. Direct, outcome-first, one idea per piece. No motivational or salesy tone — this reads as one specialist talking to another, not a charity or a coaching brand.

## Anti-references

Not a charity site, not a coaching/motivational platform, not salesy. No em dashes anywhere. No v2 palette remnants (clay, warm gold, ink-black #1D1C21) or fonts outside Outfit/Archivo. No flat black or pure-black shadows on dark surfaces — always the cooled forest gradient and `rgba(29,28,33,...)` shadows per `brand-guidelines.md`.

## Design Principles

- Outcome-first: lead with what changes, not with methodology.
- Peer-to-peer credibility over persuasion — the copy and visuals should read as expertise, not a pitch.
- One idea per piece; don't overload a section.
- The live site (krispierce.com.au) and `brand-guidelines.md` / `_ds/` are the source of truth — extend the existing v3 system rather than introducing new visual language.
- Australian English throughout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No WCAG level or specific user needs documented yet. Default to WCAG 2.1 AA (contrast ≥4.5:1 body text, visible focus states, reduced-motion support) until the user specifies otherwise.
