# Instagram (visual-led captions and carousels)

Instagram is the human and visual face of the practice. It reaches people who will never read a policy thread but who will trust a face, a moment from the work, and a clear idea told warmly. It is a feeder and a trust-builder, not the primary sales channel.

## What Instagram is for

- Putting a human entry point on the work: a photo from a workshop, a speaking moment, a desk in the middle of a project, a portrait.
- Translating one idea from the work into something a non-specialist audience can hold.
- Showing the texture of real participatory work, the behind-the-scenes that proves it is not a slide deck.

## The format hierarchy

The image earns the stop. The caption carries the substance. Lead with the human or visual entry point, then the idea.

- **Single image plus caption:** a moment plus one idea. The most common format.
- **Carousel:** for an idea that benefits from steps or a before-and-after. Slide one is the hook and must work as a standalone. One point per slide. Last slide is the so-what and a light next step.
- **Reels:** when Kris is comfortable on camera. A single idea, said to camera, under a minute. Talking-head over trends.

## Caption structure

1. **First line:** the entry point, human or concrete. This shows above the fold. Make it a reason to expand.
2. **The idea:** two to four short paragraphs developing one thought. Warmer and more plain-spoken than LinkedIn, same substance.
3. **The point:** what it means, lightly. Positioning by demonstration, not declaration.
4. **Close:** an invitation to engage or save. Honest, never "double tap if you agree".

## Format notes

- Captions can run longer than people expect; depth is welcome if it earns attention. But still one idea.
- Hashtags: a small, relevant set, placed at the end or in the first comment. Mix sector tags with a few discovery tags.
- Alt text on every image for accessibility, which also matters given the audience.
- Faces and real settings outperform stock and graphics. Authenticity is the whole point of this channel.

## Voice on Instagram

Warm, direct, human. Still an expert, just speaking to a wider room. The shift is in warmth and accessibility, not in lowering the substance. No inspirational-quote register, no motivational captions.

## Guardrails on Instagram

- Lived experience can ground a caption, but it is context, never sympathy bait and never the headline of a sales post.
- No named products or sponsors; keep industry content general.
- One idea per post.
- Run the master checklist, including the em dash check, before posting.
