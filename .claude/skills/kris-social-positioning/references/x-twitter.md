# X / Twitter (short takes and real-time commentary)

X is for speed and sharpness. It is where Kris reacts to sector news, floats a single take, and runs the occasional tight thread. It is a feeder to LinkedIn and Substack, not the place the business is won. Keep effort proportionate.

## What X is for

- A single sharp take on consumer engagement, co-design, HTA, or rare disease policy while it's current.
- Tight threads that unpack one idea from a longer Substack or LinkedIn piece.
- Being visible in the conversations that her peers and a few key journalists and policy people are already having.

## The single post

- One idea, said plainly, in one or two sentences. The constraint is the value; it forces the point.
- Lead with the claim, not the windup. The strongest line goes first.
- A standalone observation should make sense without context. Assume the reader is scrolling fast.

## Threads

Use a thread only when one idea genuinely needs steps to land. Not for a list of unrelated thoughts.

1. **Post 1:** the whole argument in miniature. It must work alone; most people only read this one.
2. **Posts 2 to 5:** one move per post. Evidence, the contrast between real and tokenistic, the implication.
3. **Final post:** the so-what, and a quiet pointer to the fuller piece (Substack or LinkedIn) if there is one.

Keep threads short. Four to six posts is usually plenty. If it needs ten, it is a Substack piece.

## Format notes

- Plain text. Minimal hashtags, one or two at most, and only if they carry weight.
- No threadbait ("a thread \U0001F9F5") unless there genuinely is a thread worth the promise.
- Link out in a reply, not the first post, when reach matters.
- Quote-tweet to add a take, not just to amplify. The added line should be the reason to share.

## Voice on X

The same expert-talking-to-peers register as everywhere else, just faster and tighter. Dry is fine. Confident is fine. Hot-take-for-engagement is not. She does not perform outrage and she does not subtweet.

## Guardrails on X

- One idea per post, even more strictly than elsewhere; the format tempts scatter.
- No motivational or inspirational posting.
- No named products or sponsors; pharma-adjacent takes stay general.
- Lived experience grounds a point occasionally; it is never bait.
- Run the master checklist, including the em dash check, before posting.
